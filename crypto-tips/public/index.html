What do BUY, HOLD, SELL and the numbers mean?
  • BUY The coin is in an uptrend and conditions look good. STRONG BUY means an uptrend plus a dip.
  • HOLD Mixed signals. If you own it, sit tight. If you don't, wait.
  • SELL The coin is in a downtrend. If you don't own it, this simply means stay out for now.
  • The signals follow the trend. They are automated, often wrong, and never a promise. Each coin shows how they did in the past.

The technical terms

  • Candle: one bar of price history. On daily candles, each bar is one day.
  • Moving average (50 / 200): the average price over the last 50 or 200 candles. Price above the 200 average means a long-term uptrend.
  • RSI: a 0 to 100 gauge of how fast the price has moved. Under 30 is "oversold", over 70 is "overbought".
  • MACD: a momentum gauge. When its line crosses above its signal line, momentum is turning up.
  • Bollinger bands: the price's usual recent range. Outside the bands means an unusually big move.
  • Score: each indicator adds or removes points. The total decides BUY, HOLD or SELL. Tap any coin to see the full breakdown.

Social buzz

What people are posting, so you can spot the hype.

How to use thisEach row shows how people feel about a coin. Hype tells you how loud the talk is. The green bar is the share of posts that are bullish (expecting the price to rise). Tap a coin to read the posts. Very high hype is a reason to be careful, not to buy.
These are opinions from strangers, not facts. Hype often peaks right before a drop, and "pump and dump" groups use posts to push prices. Use this to see the mood, not as a reason to buy.
