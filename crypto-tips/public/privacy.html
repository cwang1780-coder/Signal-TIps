Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026. This covers the Signal Tips website (signal-tips.vercel.app) and the Signal Tips iPhone app, which shows the same service.

The short version

  • No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Most of what you enter (your watchlist, the coins you hold, alerts, chart drawings, settings) stays on your device.
  • We keep the minimum on our server to run accounts and notifications, and you can delete it.

What stays on your device

Chart drawings and practice trades stay only on your device. Your watchlist, holdings, wallets, alerts and settings are also saved on your device, and copied to your account (see below).

What we keep on our server

  • Your account: you need one to use Signal Tips. We keep your email address and username, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password (never the password itself), your plan, and what you set up while signed in so it's on every device: your watchlist, alerts, the coins you've typed in, connected wallets' public addresses, prices you entered and your settings. We also note when you were last active, how many visits and sign-ins, your device type (for example "iPhone · Safari") and your approximate location (city, region and country, worked out from your internet address; never GPS). We keep your internet addresses only as one-way fingerprints, so they can be blocked if an account is banned for breaking the rules, plus a shortened form (for example 203.0.113.x). The site owner can see this in an admin panel to run the site and help you.
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple: we receive your email address and an account ID from them, and nothing else (no contacts, no password). If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only see the private relay address.
  • If you turn on notifications: your device's notification address (a web push address or an Apple push token), the symbols of the coins you hold, and the price you paid for them, so we can tell you when to sell or take profit. We do not store how much of each coin you hold. Turning notifications off deletes this.
  • If you subscribe to Pro: payments are handled by Stripe. We keep your Stripe customer and subscription IDs to know you're a subscriber; we never see or store your card details.

Information that passes through but isn't stored

  • Wallet addresses you connect are looked up on public blockchains through our server to show a balance, then forgotten. We never ask for a recovery phrase or private key, and never will.
  • Questions you ask the AI, with a snapshot of what the app is showing you (prices, signals and the coins you hold), are sent to the AI provider to get an answer: Google (Gemini) or Anthropic (Claude). On Google's free tier, Google may use what is sent to improve its products, so don't put personal details in your questions. We count how many questions you've asked this month, but we don't keep them.
  • Market data comes from public sources (Coinbase, CoinGecko, GeckoTerminal and public social sites). Your device or our server asks them for prices and posts; we don't send them anything about you.

Cookies

We use one cookie, only when you sign in, to keep you signed in. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Deleting your data

  • Delete your account under Me with Delete my account. This removes your account and synced settings from our server.
  • Turn off notifications in the My coins section to remove your notification address and coin list.
  • Clearing your browser data, or deleting the app, removes everything stored on your device.

Children

Signal Tips is not meant for anyone under 18 and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Not financial advice

Signal Tips shows automated technical signals for information only. It isn't financial advice, it doesn't trade for you, and crypto can lose all of its value.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes we'll update the date at the top. For questions or data requests, open an issue at github.com/cwang1780-coder/Signal-TIps.
